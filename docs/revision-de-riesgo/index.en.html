Which accounts should the risk team review?

A risk team can review 5% of the batch. This evaluation measures how many observed defaults would enter that queue in a historical Taiwanese credit card cohort.

Promote the candidate

The active policy uses Calibrated gradient boosting. At the same capacity, the rule based on the most recent payment delay surfaced 215 observed defaults.

The difference in average precision (AP) between boosting and logistic regression was 0.0189, with a paired 95% confidence interval of 0.0045 – 0.0322. The promotion criterion was fixed before evaluation on the test set.

Among the top 300 accounts, logistic regression surfaced 232 observed defaults. The confidence interval for the active policy's lift over the baseline rule was 0.9864 – 1.1408 (1 indicates a tie). Queue concentration and overall ranking quality answer different questions.

Publish or suspend a batch

The historical batch passed monitoring. Multiplying the credit limits by 12 in a test copy triggered a credit limit distribution shift (shift:LIMIT_BAL). In the delayed-label scenario, outcomes are pending on day 7 and can be evaluated on day 30.

October 2005 is the default window. Estimating the effect of an intervention or validating the policy for Colombia would require new evidence.
