What changes the decision

Recommending the reminder requires a lower bound above one percentage point for payments, a positive lower bound for cash and an upper bound below 0.5 percentage points for extra complaints.

When treatment outcomes go missing

pending → invalid → new run with corrected data

The first check happens before all outcomes are available. At the final check, the damaged copy is missing 3,250 outcomes. The joined data contain 10,042 control accounts and 6,708 treatment accounts (sample ratio mismatch p = 2.44e-146). The evaluator blocks the estimate. The corrected run preserves the assignment and links back to the incident.

How often does the method make the right call?

There are 1,000 independent replications per scenario. Approximate power to detect a 2 pp difference from zero is 93.5%. The recommendation also accounts for the business threshold, cash, complaints and the assignment check.
